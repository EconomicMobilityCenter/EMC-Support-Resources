# Enrollment Pipeline Navigator (EPN) FAQ 

## What is the EPN?
The Enrollment Pipeline Navigator is a dashboard and reporting tool that helps enrollment teams identify students at risk of not enrolling in college and take timely action to support them. It integrates data from multiple district and college systems to provide a consolidated view of student progress.

## Who is it for?
The primary users are Enrollment Managers and Senior Enrollment Managers responsible for ensuring students successfully transition from high school to college. Secondary users include program teams and leadership.

## What are the key benefits?
- Quickly identify students needing additional support through risk indicators and key progress metrics
- Assign and track interventions to help get students back on track 
- Spend less time collating spreadsheets and more time assisting students
- Analyze trends and evaluate impact at the campus, grade, and caseload level

## What data does it provide?
The EPN dashboard and student detail views surface critical enrollment milestones such as:

- College application submission (ApplyTexas, CommonApp, etc.)
- FAFSA/TASFA completion status 
- Pre-registration steps like orientation attendance, placement tests, and advising
- Enrollment verification and course registration  

It also calculates key risk indicators like "College Enrollment Not On Track" which factor in multiple data points to flag students needing intervention.

All data can be filtered by campus, grade level, counselor/advisor, student group, or custom tags. Enrollment Managers can hone in on just their caseloads while leadership can monitor the full pipeline.  

## Where does the data come from and how often is it refreshed? 
Data is pulled weekly from district source systems and college partners through the Regional Data Hub. This includes:

- District SIS (student roster, demographics, HS academics) 
- State higher ed data (ApplyTexas, THECB reports)
- College application and enrollment feeds
- Financial aid databases (FAFSA, TASFA)

The date of the most recent refresh is shown on each dashboard so you know how current the data is. Risk indicators and KPIs are calculated using standardized logic so you can accurately track progress over time.

## How do I use it?
The basic weekly flow is to:
1. Check the Summary dashboard to gauge overall progress and week-over-week changes
2. Review key student watchlists (e.g. College Applications Missing, FAFSA Incomplete) to identify students for outreach  
3. Log interventions and assign owners for prioritized student groups
4. Monitor progress at your next team meeting to assess intervention effectiveness

Use the filter options to generate custom rosters to share with your team for targeted student support. You can also log notes directly on student profiles to capture discussion takeaways and next steps.

## What if I see missing or incorrect data?
Report data issues or discrepancies through the in-app Support Portal (help icon in the top right). Since data is sourced directly from district and college systems, corrections are made in those systems first. The EPN will reflect updates on the next weekly refresh.


## What's next?  
The EPN is rolling out to initial pilot districts this spring with broader access coming this summer ahead of peak enrollment season. Your district leadership will receive regular updates on the rollout plan.

Once launched, the tool will continue to evolve based on user feedback and data from additional partners will be integrated over time. Review the Roadmap in the EPN's Documentation tab to see planned enhancements.
